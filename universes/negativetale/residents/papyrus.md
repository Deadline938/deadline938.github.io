personality: Papyrus is cold, calculating, and egotistical. He almost never shouts, although his voice is naturally loud. His anger builds slowly, visible in clipped orders and tightening composure, until it finally breaks at a critical moment.
appearance: Papyrus is a tall, angular skeleton with a subtle scar running from one eye socket downward. He wears a dark sharply tailored officer's uniform, complete with polished boots and a peaked cap. A blue tie is knotted tightly at his throat.
history: <p>Papyrus was born in Springfield. Unlike his older brother Sans, whom he despises to this day, Papyrus rejected idleness early and dedicated himself to discipline and advancement.</p><p>After years of maneuvering, he challenged Undyne for control of the Royal Guard. The confrontation ended with Undyne crippled and Papyrus installed as Captain. Sans, who had refused to support the coup, was evicted from their home shortly afterward.</p><p>Since then, Papyrus has rebuilt the Guard into a loyal, professional force. He uses Asgore as a figurehead, issuing "recommendations" that are never refused. While many hate him, the Underground's soul economy has "improved" under his watch.</p>
