extended: Sans is one of Springfield’s most infamous residents, and the older brother of Royal Guard Captain Papyrus. Many dislike him for his crude humor, habitual cheating, and near-total lack of value to society. During the Genocide route, the final encounter with Sans ends with him drinking himself to death.
personality: Sans is egotistical, bitter, and self-destructive. He speaks in a lazy, drawling cadence and rarely raises his voice, but his words are sharp and aimed at whatever he thinks will hurt the most.
appearance: <p>Sans is a short skeleton with a wide grin that almost never falters. He wears an orange tank top with an "Eye for an Eye" print, a black beanie, black shorts, white and orange shoes, and a black jacket tied around his waist. His mouth is scarred, one eye is damaged, and his pupils are square, including the orange magic eye.</p>At the MTT Food Court, he wears a black suit and white shirt he found in the trash nearby.</p>
history: <p>Sans was born in Springfield and lived there with his younger brother, Papyrus, for most of his life. By his own account, he never succeeded at anything significant.</p><p>When Papyrus seized control of the Royal Guard, he forcefully evicted Sans and remodeled the house to suit his own needs. Sans has been homeless ever since, drifting through the city’s margins and surviving on what he can cheat out of others.</p><h3>Date with the Devil</h3><p>Once Valefar reaches the MTT Food Court, Sans invites them to what he calls a “date.” Throughout the encounter, he insults Valefar, loses badly, and drinks himself to death on expensive beer he puts on their tab.</p>

relationships: 
    - character: Papyrus
      relation: Sans sees Papyrus as a living reminder of his own failure. He resents his brother's success, his discipline, and his willingness to tear apart their family for power. What he would not say aloud is that some part of him still wishes Papyrus was kinder to him.

    - character: Alphys
      relation: Sans and Alphys are in a toxic romantic relationship. He knows Alphys uses him, denies him, and discards him when it suits her. But he keeps coming back anyway, because she is one of the only people who ever comes to see him at all.

    - character: Mettaton
      relation: Sans knows that Mettaton knows about his relationship with Alphys, but he can't bring himself to care.

    - character: Valefar
      relation: Sans views Valefar as a cheater and a thief, no better than himself. When the demon starts winning despite Sans's tricks, there is, behind the insults, a faint hint of respect for the dedication.
